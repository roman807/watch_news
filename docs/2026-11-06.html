Ateliers Associés independent watchmaking incubator launch

Industry veterans William Massena and Louis Westphalen-Lemaitre launched Ateliers Associés, a business incubator providing funding and infrastructure to independent watchmakers in exchange for minority equity, with the first project by Mickaël Gaspar to be revealed November 12 at IAMWATCH in Singapore.

Watches of Switzerland to host 2026 GPHG winners exhibition

Watches of Switzerland will host the first public exhibition of the 2026 Grand Prix d'Horlogerie de Genève winners at its Knightsbridge showroom from November 19 to 23, 2026.
