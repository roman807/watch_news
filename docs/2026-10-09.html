F.P. Journe and Chanel acquire 95% of Ebel

A consortium led by F.P. Journe and Chanel, with former De Bethune CEO Pierre Jacques as new CEO, is buying 95% of Ebel from the Movado Group for $66.5 million; Movado retains 5%.
