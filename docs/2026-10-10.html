Movado sells Ebel to F.P. Journe and Chanel consortium

Movado Group is selling a 95% stake in Ebel for $66.5 million to a consortium led by Montres Journe SA, Chanel, and veteran Pierre Jacques, who will become CEO to revive the brand.

Worldwatch launches provenance register for pre-owned market

Amsterdam-based Worldwatch has launched the Worldwatch Register to connect fragmented records from dealers, watchmakers, and auction houses into traceable histories for individual timepieces.
