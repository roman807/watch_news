Brands Covered

Microbrands

Albishorn — Voisin Aerosport

First time-only model, retro-futuristic/Art Deco dress chronometer inspired by Gabriel Voisin. 38mm hand-wound AMT-based chronometer with layered sector dial and asymmetric seconds hand. Priced under CHF 4,000.

Auret — Mederi

Debut watch from a Colombian independent brand. 40mm steel case with a dodecagonal bezel and concentric ripple-textured dial. Decorated Sellita SW260 automatic; priced at EUR 1,750.

Monochrome: First Look – Auret Mederi, A Distinctive Debut Watch from a Colombian Independent Brand
Awake — Sơn Mài Silver Leaf Vignette

Expanded collection with four gradient vignette dials using Vietnamese lacquer and silver leaf; Purple Viola limited to 200 pieces and a permanent Indian Red variant. Redesigned 38mm steel case with 100m WR and Maison Jean Rousseau straps.

Fratello: The Awake Sơn Mài Collection Adds Bold Silver Leaf Vignette Dials
Venezianico — Nereide Opale Blu

Limited-edition diver with a lab-grown blue opal dial, new Sansovino bracelet, exhibition caseback, and mirrored tungsten bezel. 42mm case.

Independents

Laurent Ferrier — Grand Sport Tourbillon Iceberg

Grade 5 titanium 44mm case with opaline white dial and ice-blue gradients. The LF619.01 tourbillon is concealed from the dial side and visible only through the sapphire caseback. 100m water resistance with integrated bracelet.

Fratello: Laurent Ferrier Grand Sport Tourbillon Iceberg: Cool And Concealed
MB&F — LM101 Gabriel Colliard

Trilogy of limited editions (24 pieces total; eight each of red macaw, blue monkey, and green crocodile) in 40mm platinum with cloisonné enamel jungle scenes integrated into the dial and ratchet wheel. Dial plate thinned and hands extended to accommodate the enamel work. Priced at $131,000.

Ming — 51.01 Halo

37mm dress watch in crystallized grade-5 titanium. Time-only display with a manually wound La Joux-Perret caliber and a 90-hour power reserve. Blends traditional proportions with Ming’s signature avant-garde material use.
